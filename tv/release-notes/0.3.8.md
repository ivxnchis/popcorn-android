Fixes the app freezing a few seconds after it opens, and makes problems easy to report.

## Fixes
- The app froze for up to 20 seconds a few seconds after opening, and sometimes closed after that. It was looking up the current movie and TV servers in a way that blocked the screen. The lookup now runs in the background, and only once a week as intended.

## Troubleshooting
If the app crashes or misbehaves, reopen it, then on your phone or computer open the address shown on the **Open link** screen and choose **App logs** at the bottom of the page. You can read them there or download them as a file to share. If you can't get to that screen, the address is `http://` followed by the TV's IP address and `:8090`, for example `http://192.168.1.20:8090`. The IP address is in the TV's network settings.

The logs include the device and app version, the last crashes and the app's recent log. Anyone on your network can open that page while Popcorn Time is open, like the rest of it.

## Installing
Download the **arm** APK (almost every TV box, Chromecast with Google TV, Fire TV, Shield) or the **x86** one for Intel/AMD devices, for example with the Downloader app, and open it to install.

If an earlier version from these releases is installed, uninstall it first: every release is signed with a new key until the repository has its own.
