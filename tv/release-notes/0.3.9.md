The app logs now say where and why the app froze, so freezes can be fixed too.

## What's new
- When the app stops responding for more than 2 seconds, it saves what it was busy with at that moment, the screen it was on and how long the freeze lasted. The last 10 freezes are kept.
- The logs now list every screen opened, and the screen each crash happened on.
- On Android 11 and later, the logs also show why the app closed the last few times, including when Android closed it for not responding, for a crash in the video player or torrent engine, or for running out of memory.

## Sending logs
After the app crashes or freezes, reopen it if needed. Then on your phone or computer, open the address shown on the **Open link** screen and choose **App logs** at the bottom of the page. You can read them there, or download them as a file to share. If you can't get to that screen, the address is `http://` followed by the TV's IP address and `:8090`, for example `http://192.168.1.20:8090`. The IP address is in the TV's network settings.

Crashes and freezes are kept until the app is uninstalled. The rest of the log only goes back a few minutes, so get the logs soon after something goes wrong. Anyone on your network can open that page while Popcorn Time is open, like the rest of it.

## Installing
Download the **arm** APK (almost every TV box, Chromecast with Google TV, Fire TV, Shield) or the **x86** one for Intel/AMD devices, for example with the Downloader app, and open it to install.

If an earlier version from these releases is installed, uninstall it first: every release is signed with a new key until the repository has its own.
