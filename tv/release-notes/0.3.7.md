Open magnet links on your TV without typing them.

## What's new

**Send links from your phone or computer**
While Popcorn Time is open on the TV, open the address shown on its new **Open link** screen (or scan the QR code there) in a browser on your phone or computer. Paste a magnet link or choose a .torrent file, and it starts playing on the TV. Bookmark the address to skip the QR code next time.

**Open links from a browser on the TV**
Magnet links, links to .torrent files and downloaded .torrent files now open straight into the player, including through the browser's Share menu.

**More ways in on the TV**
The **Open link** screen in the "More" row also lets you type or paste a link or info hash (the Google TV app on your phone can paste into it), paste from the clipboard, or open a .torrent file from Downloads.

## Fixes
- Movies and TV shows load from the current Popcorn Time API servers.
- Magnet links opened from a browser lost their trackers.
- The player crashed on magnet links when a default subtitle language was set.
- Opening a new link while something was playing could stop the new stream.

## Installing
Download the **arm** APK (almost every TV box, Chromecast with Google TV, Fire TV, Shield) or the **x86** one for Intel/AMD devices, for example with the Downloader app, and open it to install.

If the official Popcorn Time app is installed, uninstall it first: Android won't install an app signed by someone else over it.
